# Logo

## Why is it shit?
It is just so incredibly ugly. It's so obvious that it was made by a kid in Paint. It was supposed to be a gear, but it honestly looks more like a deformed gray egg with a black cross right in the center.
![autag_logo](assets/autag_logo.png)

## How to stop being shit
I can't. It's just too ugly to even try saving. Some things are simply beyond repair, and I just can't fix it. 

But if you actually want your project to look decent and avoid my tragic fate, stop using Paint. Today, we have amazing free tools like Figma, Canva, or SVG editors. You don't even need to be a designer, just a clean and better than radioactive egg I drew back then. Accept your design limitations early.
