# Obsessed for some reason

## Why is it shit?
Basically, the number of downloads was way too important to me at that age. I still remember checking the npm page 3 to 6 times a day just to see the download count. I even remember when I reached the "huge" milestone of ~150 downloads—I was at my absolute peak of satisfaction. 

The problem with this obsession is that it severely reduced the quality of the code. Because I was desperately trying to keep my "community" active (even though probably 90% of those downloads were just bots), I kept rushing and releasing way too many versions in a very short time frame just to see the numbers go up.

## How to stop being shit
In the world of open-source, it’s incredibly easy to had bot traffic, automated package mirrors, and CI/CD pipelines for real, adoring users. 

Releasing half-baked updates every two days just to feed your ego or trick an algorithm is a fast track to writing terrible code. Shift your focus from *how many* people (or bots) are downloading your project to *how well* your project actually solves a real problem. If you build something truly useful, stable, and well-documented, real adoption will follow naturally.
